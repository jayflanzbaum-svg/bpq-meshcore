Welcome to the N4SFL Mesh Gateway

Ham radio services over MeshCore, run from South Florida.

What this is

A gateway radio ("N4SFL-8 Mesh Gateway") connects the MeshCore mesh to a ham radio packet node. From any MeshCore radio you can send it a direct message and get weather, band activity, repeater lookups, directions and an offline AI helper. Approved hams can also use the BBS (mail and bulletins that reach hams nationwide, plus Winlink email).

Getting started

  1. Add the gateway as a contact (it adverts itself every few hours) and send it a direct message. Anything works, for example ?.
  2. It answers with a menu, such as WX | SPOTS | RPTSRCH | DIRECTIONS | OFFGRIDAI. Send a command name to open that service.
  3. Long answers come in parts. Send M for more.
  4. Send BYE to leave a service and return to the menu.

What you can send

? menu   M more   BYE log off
WX weather   SPOTS band activity   RPTSRCH repeaters
DIRECTIONS driving directions   OFFGRIDAI offline AI helper
BBS mail and bulletins (approved hams)

Access levels

  • Guest: everyone. Weather, band activity, repeaters, directions, offline AI.
  • Approved ham: send MYCALL <your callsign>. The gateway asks your name, city and state and an optional email, the sysop checks your license, and you get a message back when you are approved. You can turn your radio off after you send your answers; the reply is retried until your radio is back on the air.

Good habits

  • Mesh airtime is shared. Keep messages short and don't repeat a command if the answer is slow; replies can take a minute across several hops.
  • Use a direct message, not a public channel, to talk to the gateway.
  • If you hear nothing, check that your radio has a path to a repeater and try again later.
